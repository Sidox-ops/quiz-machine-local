# Code of conduct

We want participation in Quiz Machine Local to be respectful, constructive, and
safe. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment;
- give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's private information;
- avoid discriminatory, sexualized, threatening, or deliberately disruptive
  behavior;
- follow maintainer requests intended to protect users, contributors, or the
  project.

Unacceptable behavior may result in edited or removed contributions, temporary
restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Report conduct concerns
privately through the maintainer contact method shown on the repository profile.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
