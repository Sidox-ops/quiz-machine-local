PRIVACY

Quiz Machine requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics,
telemetry, crash reporting, or publisher-operated API. Imported corpora,
generated questions, answers, scores, settings, indexes, and Ollama responses
are stored on your computer.

The default configuration sends certification and objective search terms to the
public Microsoft Learn MCP service. It does not intentionally send imported
corpora, answers, scores, question-bank content, or personal identifiers there.

The supported Ollama configuration uses its local API at 127.0.0.1. If you
configure a remote Ollama endpoint, content sent to it is governed by that
endpoint and your configuration.

Uninstalling the application may leave local data behind. Delete the Quiz
Machine application-data directory to remove imports, caches, questions,
indexes, settings, and learning progress.
